Why "AI-predicted numbers" don't work
The short version: a fair lottery draw is independent and uniformly random. Every combination is exactly as likely on Saturday as it was last Saturday, no matter what an LSTM, a Random Forest, or a "hot numbers" chart says. Machine learning models trained on past draws are fitting noise — a well-known problem sometimes used in ML courses as a cautionary example of overfitting and spurious pattern-finding. The legitimate uses of AI here are fraud detection, retailer-level anomaly flags, and analyzing player behavior — not forecasting winning numbers.
